rewrote the one-line

in src/mcp_workspace/server.py into a parenthesized multi-line form. The
statement is 76 characters, so under --profile=black (line length 88,

length, and the CI isort job failed with "Imports are incorrectly sorted
and/or formatted".

Collapse the statement back to its origin/main form. No functional change:
import ordering, the wrapped get_reference_project_path /
get_reference_repo_url group, the separate "register as" statement, and the
TYPE_CHECKING-guarded and function-local lazy github_operations imports are
all unaffected.

Note: the locally installed isort disagrees with CI here and rewrites the
statement back to the multi-line form. CI pins isort>=9.0.1; the local
environment must be upgraded to match before running ./tools/format_all.sh
on this file, otherwise the formatter reintroduces the failure.
